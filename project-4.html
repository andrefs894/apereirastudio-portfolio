Maison Belle

Services Brand Identity, Collateral
Year 2023
Industry Interior Design

The Challenge

Maison Belle is a boutique interior design studio specializing in residential spaces. They sought a brand identity that would convey sophistication, attention to detail, and the warmth they bring to every home they design.

The Approach

Drawing inspiration from French design heritage and contemporary minimalism, we created a visual identity that feels both classic and fresh. The name itself evokes beauty and home, which guided our design decisions.

We developed a refined color palette of soft neutrals accented with deep navy, paired with elegant serif typography that speaks to the studio's attention to craft and detail.

The Result

A complete brand identity including logo, secondary marks, stationery system, client presentation templates, and marketing materials. The identity has helped position Maison Belle as a premier destination for discerning homeowners.
