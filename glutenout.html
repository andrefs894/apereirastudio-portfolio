✦ O Processo

Como desenvolvemos este projeto

01

O Desafio

Alma Café is a specialty coffee shop that wanted to stand out in a competitive market while staying true to their core values of warmth, authenticity, and community.

02

A Abordagem

We developed a visual identity centered around the concept of "soul" — the literal translation of "Alma" in Portuguese. The brand reflects the warmth of a cup of coffee shared between friends.

03

O Resultado

A cohesive brand system that includes logo, typography, color palette, packaging design, and environmental graphics. The identity has helped Alma Café establish a strong presence.
