Flora Botanicals

Services Brand Identity, Packaging
Year 2024
Industry Beauty & Wellness

The Challenge

Flora Botanicals is a natural skincare line that needed a brand identity reflecting their commitment to organic ingredients and sustainable practices. The challenge was to create something that felt luxurious yet accessible, natural yet sophisticated.

The Approach

Inspired by botanical illustrations and the delicate beauty of nature, we developed a visual language that celebrates the organic origins of each product. The identity combines elegant typography with hand-drawn botanical elements.

The muted, earthy color palette reflects the natural ingredients, while subtle touches of sage green add a fresh, modern edge to the overall look.

The Result

A refined brand identity system that includes packaging design, labels, marketing materials, and digital assets. The cohesive visual language has helped Flora Botanicals establish themselves as a premium yet approachable skincare brand.
