Alma Café

Services Brand Identity, Logo Design
Year 2024
Industry Food & Beverage

The Challenge

Alma Café is a specialty coffee shop that wanted to stand out in a competitive market while staying true to their core values of warmth, authenticity, and community. They needed a brand identity that would feel both approachable and premium.

The Approach

We developed a visual identity centered around the concept of "soul" — the literal translation of "Alma" in Portuguese. The brand reflects the warmth of a cup of coffee shared between friends and the care that goes into every brew.

The color palette draws from natural coffee tones, complemented by warm terracotta accents that evoke the cozy atmosphere of the café space.

The Result

A cohesive brand system that includes logo, typography, color palette, packaging design, and environmental graphics. The identity has helped Alma Café establish a strong presence in their local community and build a loyal customer base.
