Nora Studio

Services Visual Identity, Art Direction
Year 2023
Industry Creative Services

The Challenge

Nora Studio is a photography and creative direction studio that needed a visual identity as refined and artistic as their work. They wanted something minimal yet memorable, timeless yet contemporary.

The Approach

We embraced a philosophy of elegant restraint. The visual identity relies on perfect proportions, thoughtful spacing, and a monochromatic palette that allows their photography to take center stage.

Typography plays a crucial role, with a custom logotype that balances geometric precision with subtle organic curves, reflecting the studio's approach to their craft.

The Result

A sophisticated visual identity system that includes logo variations, stationery, portfolio templates, and social media assets. The understated elegance has become synonymous with Nora Studio's creative excellence.
